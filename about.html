About DataSync Solutions

DataSync Solutions provides software and data services to housing associations and local authorities across the UK. We focus on helping organisations get the most from their data — through expert migrations, reliable integrations, and insightful reporting.

With years of experience in SQL Server, Azure, Power BI, and ETL processes, we’ve supported multiple large-scale transformations and system upgrades in the housing sector.

We pride ourselves on precision, transparency, and collaboration — ensuring every project delivers measurable value and long-term success.
